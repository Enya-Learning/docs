## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Enya Learning?">
    Enya Learning is a learning platform for students, educators, and families. It brings courses and role-specific learning tools together in one place.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who is this help center for?">
    This help center is for students, educators, and parents using Enya Learning. Choose the guide above that best matches your role.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I get started?">
    If you already have an account, sign in through your Enya Learning sign-in page. If you are new, start the sign-up flow and select your role. See the [Getting started guide](/guides/getting-started) for an overview.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I forgot my password. How can I reset it?">
    On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot Password?** and follow the on-screen steps to reset your password.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I join a classroom?">
    Open **Classrooms**, choose **Join Classroom**, and enter the code for your class. Follow the [joining a classroom guide](/guides/tutorials/joining-a-classroom) if you need a walkthrough.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can I find guides inside Enya Learning?">
    Students, educators, and parents have a **Guides** area in their Enya Learning dashboard. This help center also has role-based guides at the top of this page.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does XP work?">
    XP is earned through course activities and games. See the [XP guide](/guides/tutorials/how-xp-works) for the current rules and where to find them in the app.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
