Enya Learning has different spaces for students, educators, and parents. Start with the role that matches how you use the platform.

## Sign in or get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Enya Learning">
    Open your Enya Learning sign-in page. If you already have an account, choose **Sign in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the right option">
    If you already have an account, select **Sign in**. If you are new, start the sign-up flow and select **Student**, **Educator**, or **Parent**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore your dashboard">
    Your dashboard is organized around your role. Students can explore courses and learning activities; educators can work with courses and classrooms; parents can follow their child's learning.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If you need help signing in

- If you forgot your password, choose **Forgot Password?** on the sign-in page.
- For general information, visit [Enya Learning](https://www.enyalearning.com/).
